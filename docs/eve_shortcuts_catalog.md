# EVE Shortcut Command Catalog

Extracted from the recent EVE Online Steam screenshots in the local Steam screenshots folder.

Frequency tiers are practical estimates for typical solo/new-player EVE use plus voice-command usefulness, not telemetry. `Critical` means time-sensitive or often worth saying quickly.

Total rows: 294. The sortable alphabetical CSV is `data/eve_shortcuts_catalog.csv`.

One row is marked inferred: `Toggle Overload on High Power Rack`. It appears to sit in the tiny scroll gap between the module screenshots and follows the standard rack shortcut pattern. Verify in-game before binding it.
